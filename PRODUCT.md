# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences meet on the public marketing surface (the dashboard homepage and adjacent
landing views at `autotradingportfolios.vercel.app`):

- **The owner / operator** — a quant-minded builder running three autonomous $100K paper
  portfolios on Alpaca. Visits to feel the system is alive, institutional, and worth the
  craft poured into it. Knows the internals; wants the surface to match the ambition.
- **The evaluator** — a prospective collaborator, recruiter, or peer landing cold. Has
  ~5 seconds of first impression. Should immediately read "this was built by someone
  operating at a Goldman / Bloomberg / Stripe bar," not "another AI-generated template."

Both arrive on desktop and mobile, in light or dark, in English or Arabic (RTL).

## Product Purpose

Auto Trading (by RiseWealth) is a fully autonomous, self-improving multi-portfolio
trading system. The homepage is not a transaction surface, it is a **statement of
capability**: it has to make a visitor believe the intelligence is real and continuously
working. Success is the visitor pausing, scrolling the whole page, and walking away with
"how was this made?" rather than "which AI made this?"

## Brand Personality

Three physical-object words: **molten, precise, alive.**

- **Molten** — warm solar metal, depth and glow, never cold or neon. The palette is poured
  bronze and ember, not navy-and-gold fintech, not crypto neon.
- **Precise** — institutional-grade rigor. Monospaced data, exact numbers, real Alpaca
  feeds. The polish implies the engineering.
- **Alive** — the product is autonomous and self-learning, so the surface must visibly
  breathe: perpetual, purposeful motion and a living 3D centerpiece that reacts to the
  visitor. Stillness would contradict the thesis.

Voice: confident, spare, technical without jargon-cosplay. Lowercase mono labels, oversize
serif display moments. Every word earns its place.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI SaaS landing pages**: centered stack, hero with a gradient-clipped headline,
  a row of identical icon-on-rounded-square cards, "trusted by" logo wall. This is exactly
  what the current homepage looks like and what we are killing.
- **Cold fintech**: navy + gold, glass skyscrapers, stock-photo handshakes.
- **Crypto neon-on-black**: cyber gradients, glowing grids, Orbitron type.
- **Editorial-magazine-by-reflex**: display-serif + ruled-column broadsheet with no reason
  to be a magazine. We use serif for impact, not to cosplay a publication.

## Design Principles

1. **Living system, living surface.** Autonomy is the product thesis, so the page never
   sits still. A WebGL centerpiece and orchestrated scroll choreography make the
   intelligence *felt*, not just claimed.
2. **Molten, not neon.** Warmth, depth, and glow inside the RiseWealth solar ramp. Bloom
   and emission are allowed; cold or garish color is not.
3. **Earned spectacle.** Every animation serves comprehension or brand. No motion for
   decoration. If a visitor can't say what an effect communicates, it's cut.
4. **Institutional honesty.** Only real data reaches the surface (live Alpaca quotes, real
   portfolio counts). The craft implies rigor; fabricated numbers would betray it. This is a
   hard rule inherited from the system: never fabricate equity or price data.
5. **One dominant idea per fold.** Long scroll, deliberate pacing, single-purpose
   viewports. Art direction can shift per section as long as the voice holds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast across the warm palette in both themes.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` path: the 3D scene freezes to a composed static frame,
  scroll-driven motion collapses to instant reveals, no autoplay loops, no parallax.
- RTL / Arabic parity is mandatory (existing EN/AR toggle, IBM Plex Sans Arabic).
- Keyboard operable, visible focus, semantic landmarks. Motion must never trap or block
  content for any input modality.
- WebGL is progressive enhancement: no-WebGL and low-power devices get a premium static
  hero, never a broken canvas.
